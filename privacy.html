Privacy Policy — GTA 6 Clips

Last updated: November 19, 2026.

What the app accesses

GTA 6 Clips requests two Google permissions: youtube.upload, to upload videos to the YouTube channel of the person who authorized it, and youtube.force-ssl, to change the visibility of those uploaded videos and read their view counts. It never deletes videos and never accesses other channels. Only the operator of this tool authorizes it, for their own channel.

What data is stored

The app stores an OAuth access token for the operator's own Google account on the operator's private computer, to upload videos. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person. It reads public live-stream chat only to measure message activity; it keeps only the few most frequent words of a highlight, not usernames or full messages.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok

GTA 6 Clips also uses the TikTok Content Posting API with the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish and video.list, only for the operator's own TikTok account: to post the operator's own clips (privately; the operator makes them public in the TikTok app) and to see which of them are public. The access token is stored only on the operator's private computer. No data of other TikTok users is collected. Access can be revoked in the TikTok app: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
