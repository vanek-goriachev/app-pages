GTA 6 Clips

GTA 6 Clips is a personal tool that turns highlights from public Twitch and Kick live streams into short vertical clips with subtitles and uploads them to our own YouTube channel.

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the channel owned by the operator of this tool. It does not access, collect or store data of any other YouTube users.

Every clip credits the original streamer with a link to their channel. Streamers who want a clip removed can write to an.acc.for.gta.6.clips@gmail.com, and it will be taken down.
